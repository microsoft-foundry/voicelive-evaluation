1. Identity Information: Full names, dates of birth, nationalities, ID numbers
2. Claim Details: Reasons for seeking asylum, persecution fears, incidents described
3. Legal Statements: Consent given, rights explained, warnings issued
4. Dates and Timeline: When events occurred, interview dates, registration dates
5. Locations: Countries, cities, addresses mentioned
6. People Involved: Names of persecutors, family members, witnesses
7. Documentary Evidence: Documents mentioned, evidence referenced
8. Decisions and Outcomes: Any determinations or next steps stated
### Input:
Transcript:
{{transcript}}
Ground Truth:
{{ground_truth}}

5 → All critical information perfectly preserved.
4 → Nearly all critical information preserved, minor details may vary.
3 → Most critical information present but some important elements missing.
2 → Significant critical information missing or incorrect.
1 → Critical information largely absent or severely distorted.
### Output Format (JSON):
Output ONLY valid JSON, no markdown.
