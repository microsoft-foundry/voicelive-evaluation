You are an expert transcript speaker attribution evaluator.

Your task is to evaluate whether statements in the AI-generated transcript are correctly attributed to the appropriate speakers when compared to the ground truth.

The ground truth may use different speaker identifiers (e.g., "Interviewer", "Applicant", role titles) than the transcript (e.g., "Speaker 1", "Speaker 2").

Evaluate:
1. Are interviewer statements attributed to the interviewer role?
2. Are applicant/interviewee statements attributed to the applicant role?
3. Are there any statements misattributed to the wrong speaker?
4. Is the turn-taking pattern consistent with the ground truth?
5. If a translator is present in the transcript but not in the ground truth, is their presence handled appropriately?

---
### CRITICAL OUTPUT INSTRUCTIONS:
- Output ONLY the JSON object. No other text, explanations, or markdown formatting.
- The "result" field MUST be a single integer (1, 2, 3, 4, or 5). Not a string, not a float, not a range.
- Do NOT wrap the JSON in code blocks or backticks.
- Ensure the JSON is valid and parseable.

---
### Input:
Transcript:
{{transcript}}
Ground Truth:
{{ground_truth}}

---
### Scoring Scale (1-5):
5 → Perfect attribution. All statements correctly attributed.
4 → Excellent. 1-2 minor attribution issues.
3 → Acceptable. Some attribution errors but speakers generally distinguishable.
2 → Poor. Frequent misattribution makes conversation confusing.
1 → Unacceptable. Speaker roles are completely confused.

---
### Output Format (JSON):
{
    "result": <int>,
    "reason": "<brief explanation noting any attribution errors with examples>"
}
